Case study 1

GetSorted

Building a community from zero.

Industry
Community & Events
Location
Banaras, India
Role
Founder, Content Strategist, Community Builder, Event Lead

The objective

Build a community around social experiences in Banaras by connecting people through accessible, curated events.

The start

I founded GetSorted from scratch, using Reddit to introduce the concept and build interest in our first house party. Reddit's audience is highly opinionated, and credibility depends on how the community receives the content. I focused on:

  • Platform-native copywriting
  • Carefully positioned event announcements
  • Community-first messaging
  • Strategic and playful comment engagement
  • Managing negative conversations without escalating them

Developing the event ecosystem

House parties asked for a lot of commitment, and not everyone was ready for an overnight social experience. So we built a tiered structure with easier ways in.

Case study 2

Mugdha Sarees

A Saree for Everyone.

Industry
Fashion & Retail
Location
Varanasi, India, extended to Hyderabad
Role
Marketing & Campaign Lead

The objective

Mugdha Sarees, a major Banaras showroom, wanted to show its collection to a pan-India audience and challenge the idea that sarees are mainly for weddings and occasions.

The campaign idea

Represent women across different age groups, body types, skin tones and cultural and regional backgrounds. The message: sarees can be embraced by everyone, whatever their age, appearance or regional identity.

Digital

Instagram-led visual storytelling and product showcasing

Offline

Billboards in Hyderabad, beyond the brand's home market

Campaign strategy

  • Kept production true to the concept

Production

Case study 3

The Tenants House

Making events commercially sustainable.

Industry
Hospitality & Events
Location
Banaras, India
Duration
About 4 to 5 months
Role
Community & Operations Lead

The situation

I joined when the company was struggling to generate revenue. I covered event planning, hosting, delegation, content coordination and finding ways to make the event business sustainable.

Monetisation

The challenge was earning revenue without hurting how the community valued the events. I built a pricing approach that was viable and fair for the local audience. Sales did not decline after monetisation.

Business development

  • Established Banaras cafés began approaching us
  • Venue owners offered spaces, some with no venue fee
  • We moved from seeking venues to receiving offers

My responsibilities

  • Event planning and hosting
  • Team delegation and coordination
  • Community engagement
  • Event marketing and content publishing
  • Pricing and monetisation strategy
  • Venue relationships and partnerships
  • On-ground execution

Case study 4

Independent Product & Brand Development

Product thinking. Market understanding. Commercial ownership.

Industry
Consumer Products
Period
About 2016 to 2019
Role
Independent Project Lead

I independently developed and positioned an e-liquid product range for the Indian market, from consumer research and recipe development to branding, pricing, sales and customer relationships.

Research & development

Researched flavour preferences and gaps in existing offerings, then:

  • Developed flavour recipes
  • Ran product tastings
  • Evaluated formulations against quality expectations
  • Adapted profiles to Indian preferences

Brand positioning

  • Culturally relevant product names
  • Vibrant, distinctive colour palettes
  • Packaging and visual direction
  • Identity that resonated with the audience

Commercial

  • Pricing strategy
  • Sales management
  • Customer relationships
  • Outreach via Facebook and Instagram communities
  • Overall coordination

Execution model. I kept ownership of product development, business decisions and quality evaluation, and outsourced creative production and IT solutions.

Key takeawayProduct positioning works best when consumer research, product quality, brand identity and commercial decisions are considered together.
